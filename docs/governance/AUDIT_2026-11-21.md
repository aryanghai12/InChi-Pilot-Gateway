## 2. Executive summary

1. **One Critical access-control defect (AUD-01) and one High password-handling defect (AUD-15).** Anonymous self-registration with a caller-chosen role is open in `backend/apps/accounts/`. It breaks D-12 ("no self-registration") and v2.2 §14.1, and it blocks Gate 4 ("no critical unresolved access-control defect"). Separately, users created in the Django admin get plaintext passwords.
2. **Baseline v2.2 is now the governing BNCS source for readiness and matching**, and it settles D-02, D-03, D-04, D-05, D-06, D-08, D-09 and the original D-15. Five of those settlements conflict with what the repository builds or records (§3). The largest conflict: v2.2 makes MVP matching **deterministic and rules-only with no hidden weights**, while the repository's architecture makes **semantic matching mandatory with weighted hybrid scoring**. Week 5 cannot start on matching until D-21 is decided.
3. **The previous documentation pass overstated status.** README, AGENTS, MVP_FEATURES, ARCHITECTURE and the registers said "Gate 2 Passed" and marked D-02, D-06, D-09, D-13, D-14 and D-15 as Approved or Resolved, with no approver or date. They also recorded D-02 and D-06 values that v2.2 contradicts. These are corrected in this change.
4. **Decision IDs collided.** `D-15` meant three things (business: reviewer approval of requirements; AGENTS/ARCHITECTURE: FastAPI runtime; REGISTERS: "Resolved" deployment). `D-12` meant both authentication and vector storage. The FastAPI runtime decision is now **TD-11**, and vector storage is **TD-01**.
5. **M4 and M5 are implemented but have no backend tests.** CI runs only the accounts and registry backend tests. It does not run `apps/matching`, `ai_matching`, readiness or demand tests, and it has no Ruff or OpenAPI drift check.
6. **M6 to M9 are not integrated.** Matching has an engine and a Django client, but no models, views or URLs. Dashboard, audit-history and opportunities APIs do not exist. The frontend pages for them fall back to empty states.
7. **The setup works.** `docker compose up --build` brings up all four services healthy from `.env.example` defaults. The new [IMPLEMENTATION.md](../IMPLEMENTATION.md) is written from the verified run.
