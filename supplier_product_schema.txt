# Supplier Products Schema V1 — InChi Pilot Gateway

## 1. Purpose

The `supplier_products` table connects suppliers and products through a many-to-many relationship.

It also stores supplier-specific information about each product, including its price and availability.

## 2. Table Definition

- **Table name:** `supplier_products`
- **Database:** PostgreSQL
- **Schema version:** V1
- **Primary key:** Composite primary key (`supplier_id`, `product_id`)

## 3. Field Definitions

| Field | PostgreSQL Type | Nullable | Default | Description |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `supplier_id` | UUID | No | None | References the supplier offering the product |
| `product_id` | UUID | No | None | References the product offered by the supplier |
| `price` | NUMERIC(12,2) | Yes | NULL | Price offered by this supplier for this product |
| `availability` | VARCHAR(20) | No | `available` | Availability of this product from this supplier |
| `created_at` | TIMESTAMPTZ | No | Current timestamp | When the relationship was created |
| `updated_at` | TIMESTAMPTZ | No | Current timestamp | When the relationship was last updated |

## 4. Constraints and Data Integrity

### 4.1 Composite Primary Key
- The combination of `supplier_id` and `product_id` must be unique.
- The same product cannot be linked to the same supplier more than once.
- Both fields are mandatory.

### 4.2 Foreign Keys
- `supplier_id` references `suppliers(id)`.
- `product_id` references `products(id)`.
- Foreign-key deletion behavior must be agreed upon when implementing migrations, considering historical records and auditability.

### 4.3 Price
- `price` may be NULL when the price is unknown or not yet provided.
- If present, the price must be greater than or equal to zero.
- Currency is not included in V1 and requires confirmation before prices from different currencies are compared.

### 4.4 Availability
The proposed permitted values are:
- `available`
- `unavailable`

The database should enforce the permitted values through a CHECK constraint.

Availability describes whether a particular supplier can currently provide the associated product. It does not represent the supplier's overall account status.

### 4.5 Timestamps
- `created_at` and `updated_at` must be non-null.
- Both fields default to the current database timestamp.
- The backend or a database trigger must maintain `updated_at` when a record changes.

## 5. Design Decisions

### 5.1 Many-to-Many Relationship
A supplier can offer multiple products, and a product can be offered by multiple suppliers.

The junction table represents this relationship without duplicating supplier or product records.

### 5.2 Supplier-Specific Price
Price belongs to the supplier-product relationship because different suppliers may offer the same product at different prices.

### 5.3 Supplier-Specific Availability
Availability belongs to the supplier-product relationship because a product may be available from one supplier and unavailable from another.

### 5.4 Composite Primary Key
The combination of `supplier_id` and `product_id` identifies a unique supplier-product relationship in V1.

If the system later needs multiple offers for the same product from the same supplier, this key design must be revisited.

## 6. Indexing Strategy

- The composite primary key provides an index beginning with `supplier_id`.
- An additional index on `product_id` may be useful when searching all suppliers offering a particular product.
- Additional indexes should be introduced based on actual query patterns.

## 7. Auditability

Changes to price and availability may affect supplier comparisons and matching results.

Important changes should be captured by the project's separate append-only audit mechanism. Historical matching results should remain interpretable even when the current price or availability changes.

## 8. Open Assumptions

1. Whether prices will use one currency or require an explicit currency field.
2. Whether availability should eventually include stock quantities or lead times.
3. Whether `available` and `unavailable` are sufficient status values.
4. How `updated_at` will be maintained.
5. How supplier-product records will be handled when a supplier or product is deactivated or deleted.
6. Which changes must generate audit events.

These assumptions require confirmation with the project team.
